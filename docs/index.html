Your local bin colours

Recycling rules differ between cities. Set the names and colours used where you are. The default colours are colour-blind-safe, and the bin name is always shown in words.

How it works

SortRight looks at one item and suggests the material and the bin. It runs entirely in your browser, so photos never leave your phone.

When it says "not sure", do not guess. Check your local recycling guide or ask staff. It is built to say so when the photo is unclear, the item is dirty or mixed, or it is not a type it knows.

Tips: one item per photo, item in the middle, good light, close enough to fill about half the picture.

Limits

This is an indicative assistant, not an official rule. Dirty or greasy items, mixed materials, clear plastic film, cartons and electronics are often misjudged. Rules differ by city: edit the bin names on the Bins tab.

About the models

You can switch between two models on the Scan tab. SortRight is trained for this project (13 MB). MobileCLIP-S0 is a small general-purpose vision-language model from Apple (38 MB) that works without any recycling training. Both run on your phone.
